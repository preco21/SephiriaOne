# PvP guard MP scaling plan

**Goal:** Configurable maximum-MP-relative cost for right-click guarding on balanced PvP hits across casual friendly fire, Deathmatch, Tournament and Play Tag.

**Approved semantics:** Default 10% of maximum MP. Preserve Guard Efficiency, perfect guard's half-cost, and native cost callbacks. Use current maximum MP and current settings at the final guard spend, before native guard-break handling. No change to parries, MP shields, PvE, self/system hits or non-player victims. Native behavior returns immediately when effective PvP or balancing ends. No native stat writes or per-player cached costs.

**Design:** Guard-only amount conversion inside the existing guarded `ApplyDamage` transpiler and hit scope. Cost is `ceil(nativeModifiedCost / 10 * MaxMp * GuardMpPercent / 100)` for admitted balanced PvP; zero native cost stays zero. Native unbalanced conversion remains truncation. Range 0..100%, up to two decimals; 0 explicitly waives the scaled cost. `/one friendlyfire guard-mp N|reset`, status, `/one save`; default-safe setting and v27 only for nondefault presets. Existing older presets inherit 10%. A compact Combat/PvP balancing subsection selector keeps the current window dimensions and participant list; shared KDA/revive/master controls remain visible. EN/KO translations.
